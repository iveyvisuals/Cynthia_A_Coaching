Cynthia's Story

In 2021, Cynthia, a mother, teacher, and professional dancer, suffered a severe brain injury that brought her life to a sudden stop. For years, she battled post-concussion symptoms and debilitating panic attacks as she tried to rebuild herself from the ground up. ​ Everything shifted when she learned how to create safety in her nervous system. Through a brain-injury program, she was introduced to nervous-system regulation and EFT tapping, tools that changed the trajectory of her recovery.

Step by step, Cynthia reclaimed her life. She went on to build two successful businesses, returned to the stage and found presence in her parenting. Today, she shares these same high-impact practices with high-performing teams, athletes, creatives, and leaders. She brings lived experience, deep expertise, and powerful presence to every room, carrying both the weight of what a brain injury demands and the proof of what nervous-system work can make possible.

About Cynthia

Nervous-system-informed coaching, built on lived experience

Cynthia Aguiar is a certified trauma-informed embodiment coach, performance mentor, speaker, and wellness expert dedicated to helping individuals and organizations unlock their fullest potential—through a grounded, science-informed, body-first approach.

With over a decade of experience as an educator and extensive training in Coaching, Breathwork, EFT Tapping, NLP, and Somatic Movement, Cynthia has led hundreds of workshops and sessions across a wide range of settings. Her work has supported entrepreneurs, women’s groups, retreat participants, and professionals—including doctors, lawyers, IVEY business students, and international corporate teams. Her expertise is frequently called upon for global retreats, professional development programs, and leadership training.

If you're ready to lead, perform, or grow from a place of true nervous system safety—Cynthia is your guide.

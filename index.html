About Cynthia

Nervous-system-informed coaching, built on lived experience

Cynthia Aguiar is a certified trauma-informed embodiment coach, performance mentor, and speaker dedicated to helping individuals and organizations create meaningful, lasting change through a grounded, science-informed, body-first approach.

With over a decade as an educator and extensive training in coaching, breathwork, EFT tapping, NLP, and somatic movement, she's led hundreds of workshops for entrepreneurs, professionals, and corporate teams — helping people build self-awareness, regulate their nervous system, and shift the patterns keeping them stuck.

Whether you're breaking old patterns or learning to lead under pressure, Cynthia — based in London, Ontario — helps you get your body on board with the life you're building.

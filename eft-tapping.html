EFT Tapping

A five-minute tool your nervous system already knows how to use

Emotional Freedom Technique (EFT), or "tapping," is a simple, evidence-informed practice that combines gentle acupressure with focused attention to calm the body's stress response — quickly, and without any equipment.

Book a Call Get in Touch
What Is EFT Tapping?

What is it, exactly?

EFT involves lightly tapping on specific points on the face and body — the same meridian points used in acupuncture — while naming and processing a specific stress, worry or emotion out loud. The combination of physical touch and verbal acknowledgment appears to help settle the body's fight-or-flight response, often within minutes.

Who It's For, and Why

Simple enough for anyone, useful for everyone

Who

Who Will It Benefit?

Anyone who feels stress, anxiety, performance pressure, or overwhelm in their body — from students before an exam to executives before a big presentation.

What

What Are the Benefits?

Reduced anxiety in the moment, better focus under pressure, improved sleep, and a tool you can use anywhere, with no equipment and no cost.

Why

Why Do It?

Because regulating your nervous system shouldn't require a retreat or a prescription. Tapping is fast, private, and backed by a growing body of research.

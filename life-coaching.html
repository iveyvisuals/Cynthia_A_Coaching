Life Coaching

Life coaching with Cynthia isn't about forcing motivation or chasing a better mindset. It's steady, trauma-informed work that helps you understand your patterns, regulate your stress response, and build a life that feels like yours again.

What is Life Coaching?

Life coaching with Cynthia is a collaborative, one-on-one process. Sessions blend practical goal-setting with nervous-system-aware tools — including breathwork and EFT tapping where helpful — so you're not just talking about change, you're actually building the capacity to sustain it. There's no generic script. Each plan is shaped around your history, your goals, and how your body responds to stress.

Who It's For

Built for people ready to feel steadier

Cynthia works with individuals navigating burnout, life transitions, perfectionism, chronic stress, and recovery from difficult experiences — including brain injury. If you're high-functioning on the outside but running on empty, this is for you.

Who Will It Benefit?

  • Professionals managing burnout or chronic overwhelm
  • People in the middle of a major life transition
  • Anyone rebuilding confidence after a health event or brain injury
  • Leaders who want to regulate before they respond

What Are the Benefits?

  • A calmer, more regulated nervous system day to day
  • Clarity on what you actually want, not what you think you should want
  • Tools you can use long after coaching ends
  • A steady, judgment-free space to be honest
Tools for your future

Why Do It?

Most of us have tried to think our way out of stress. It rarely works, because stress isn't only a mindset problem — it's a nervous system problem. Coaching gives you a structured, supported process to actually shift the pattern, not just manage around it.
